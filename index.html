About

The CHS Architecture & Engineering Club is an exciting after-school club where creativity meets innovation! Join us to design and build engaging projects while exploring the fascinating world of engineering and architecture. Dive into various engineering fields, including mechanical, electrical, civil, computer, and architectural disciplines. Whether you're a budding engineer, an aspiring architect, or simply curious about how things work, this club is the perfect place to learn, collaborate, and bring your ideas to life.
